# Starting point: the skill being sought is routing, not recall

The learner's stated position at the start of this topic: the goal is **durable
retrieval** rather than a project needing these flows this week; the skills have
been **barely run** — installed, two or three tried; and when asked which of the
five workflows should carry the most weight, the answer was **"depends on the use
case"**.

That third answer is the one that decided the curriculum, and it is worth
recording as an insight rather than as a preference. Read as a refusal to choose
it would be unhelpful; read literally it is exactly right, and it names the skill
being sought. The difficulty with twenty-five skills and five flows is not
remembering what any one of them contains — the tree is on disk and greppable.
The difficulty is standing at the start of a piece of work and picking. So the
object of study is the **routing decision**, and the five recipes are worked
answers to it rather than the subject itself.

**Implications.** Routing cannot be taught first, even though it is the point.
The choice between `/implement` and `/to-spec` is meaningless until the learner
knows what a user-invoked skill is, and the choice to reach for `/grill-with-docs`
over `/grill-me` is meaningless until grilling itself is understood. Lesson 7 is
therefore the hinge of the topic: lessons 1–6 exist to make it legible, and
lessons 8–17 are its five branches worked through. Every flow lesson leads with
its **trigger condition** and only then gives the recipe, because the trigger is
the part that serves the mission.

**A second consequence of the near-zero starting point.** Nothing may be taught
as "you will recognise this from having run it" — and, more sharply, nothing may
be taught as what a flow *produces*, because no flow has been run by the learner
or by the agent that wrote these lessons. Every page states what a skill's text
instructs; observed behaviour is quoted from the repo's own docs pages and
labelled as a user report. Keeping those two grades apart is not caution here, it
is the only honest option available.

**Not yet established.** Nothing has been confirmed by evidence — this record
states the starting point *as stated*, not as demonstrated. The first real
session with any of these flows should produce the second learning record, and
it is likely to revise lesson 14 or lesson 16, which describe the two flows
furthest from anything the learner has done.
