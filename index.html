Transactional Outbox

Seven lessons taking the pattern from the dual-write problem to operating it in production, built for recall under questioning rather than recognition.

Read them in order the first time — each one ends by deferring something the next picks up. After that, the glossary is the page to come back to.

Lessons

  • Lesson 01 The Dual-Write Problem Why there is no safe order in which to do two writes, and what the outbox actually buys you.
  • Lesson 02 The Message Relay The table, the claim query, and the one design decision the whole relay turns on.
  • Lesson 03 At-Least-Once and the Idempotent Consumer Where every duplicate comes from, and the one transaction boundary that makes them harmless.
  • Lesson 04 Polling or Tailing the Log What change data capture buys you, what it does not, and why the bill arrives on your primary database.
  • Lesson 05 Ordering, and How Much You Need Refuse the requirement you were handed, then buy the smaller one cheaply. Carries a measured correction: single active consumer is not enough.
  • Lesson 06 Operating It Monitoring, poison messages and retention — the first two in direct conflict with the ordering you just bought.
  • Lesson 07 Run It Postgres and RabbitMQ in Docker, the relay killed with SIGKILL in the exact millisecond that matters.
